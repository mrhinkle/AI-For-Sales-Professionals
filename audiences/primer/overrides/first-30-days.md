## Your first 30 days

### Week 1 | Foundation
Desktop app, approved connectors, your instructions, memory on.

### Week 2 | A shared Drive
Templates, examples, brand voice and a skills folder.

### Week 3 | One pilot, end to end
One workflow done fully in Claude, like a renewal quote.

### Week 4 | Make it a skill
Add success criteria, share it, schedule the prep.

### Every quarter | Tune it up
Prune memory, update skills, retire what's stale.

notes:
Don't try to do everything this week. Week one is setup. Week two is the shared Drive, so the team works from the same templates and examples. Week three, pick one workflow and do it start to finish in Claude. Week four, save it as a skill with success criteria and share it. After that, the habit is a quarterly tune-up. If you get stuck, email me.
