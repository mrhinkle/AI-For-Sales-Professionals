# Deal review, the skeptic

Run it before the pipeline review, not after.

```
Here are my notes, emails and call summaries for the [Company] deal. [paste]
Act as a tough sales manager. Tell me: what we actually know versus what I'm assuming; who the real
decision maker is and whether I've talked to them; what could kill this deal in the next 30 days;
the one action that most improves our odds this week. Be blunt. Don't tell me what I want to hear.
```
