# Proposal draft

```
Using our proposal template [paste], my product file, and the transcript and notes from [Company] [paste],
draft the proposal. Restate their problem in their own words from the transcript. Map each need to
what [product] does, with the proof we can name. Leave every price, discount, and date as [TBD]
for me to fill in. Add a mutual action plan with the steps between today and signature.
Flag any promise in the draft that isn't in my product file.
```

Business case variant: "Here are the numbers they gave us on the call: [paste]. Build the business case and show every calculation step by step. Mark any number you had to assume." If it can't show the arithmetic, it made the number up.
