A tool this team would use this week, from one paragraph.

## Outreach with Claude driving and you approving

### Research
Connectors pull the CRM history, news and job posts.

### Build the list
Claude works Apollo in the browser against your ICP.

### Draft the sequence
Your voice skill writes three short emails per person.

### You send
Read the first send to each person. Then let the sequence run.

notes:
Here's the whole thread end to end. I'll run it live on one account. Research comes from the connectors. The list gets built in Apollo by Claude in the browser. The drafts come from your voice skill. And then it stops and waits for you. The automation does the sorting and the drafting. You approve the first send to every person, and every LinkedIn step is yours.
