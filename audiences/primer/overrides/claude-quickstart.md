# Set up Claude first

Twenty minutes, once. Everything after this depends on it.

notes:

## Five steps, in this order

### Step 1 | Use a paid seat
Your company's Team or Enterprise account if you have one.

### Step 2 | Install the apps
The Claude desktop app, plus Claude in Chrome for the browser.

### Step 3 | Connect your tools
Settings, then Connectors. Email, calendar and CRM first.

### Step 4 | Turn on skills
Then add your voice skill. We'll build it together today.

### Step 5 | Make one project
Your voice, ICP and product files, loaded in every chat.

notes:
Here's the whole setup on one slide, and the rest of the hour fills in each step. A paid seat matters: the company account carries the security settings your admin agreed to, and the browser extension is only on paid plans. The desktop app and the Chrome extension are where Claude does real work. Connectors are next, and they're the big one. Skills and a project are how you stop pasting the same context into every chat.
