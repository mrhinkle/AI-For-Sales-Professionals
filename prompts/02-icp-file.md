# ICP file

Run once with your sales leader's input. Save as `icp.md`.

```
Help me write a one-page ideal customer profile for [company], which sells [product] to [buyer].
Interview me: ask one question at a time about company size, industries, geographies, the titles
who buy and who influence, the technology or situation that makes them a fit, the trigger events
that make them call (hiring, funding, a new leader, an outage, a migration), and what disqualifies
a lead on sight (competitors, students, job seekers, existing customers, wrong region).
When you have enough, write the profile with a section called "Disqualify immediately if".
```
