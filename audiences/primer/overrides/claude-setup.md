# Claude gives you *superpowers*. Only if you set it up.

Out of the box it knows the internet. It doesn't know your accounts, your calendar or your pipeline.

notes:
Most people open Claude, type a question, get a generic answer and decide AI is overhyped. That's like hiring a great rep and never giving them a CRM login. The setup is twenty minutes, once. After that every chat starts with your tools, your files and your rules already loaded. That's the difference between a toy and a teammate.

## Start in Settings, not in the chat box

### Connectors | Plug in your tools
Gmail, calendar, CRM, Slack. Claude reads and drafts inside them.

### Skills | Teach it how you work
Your voice, your ICP, your call prep. Loaded only when needed.

### Projects | Keep the three files handy
Every chat in the project starts with them already read.

### Chrome | Let it use the browser
For the sites that have no connector. You approve what happens.

notes:
Four places, and we'll take them in order. Connectors are the big one. Skills are how you stop explaining yourself. Projects hold the files we'll write in a minute. And Claude in Chrome covers every tool that doesn't have a connector yet, which in sales is a lot of them. Open Settings on your own screen while I talk if you want to follow along.

## Decide what each tool is allowed to do

### Always allow | Reading and searching
Looking things up in your CRM, inbox and Drive.

### Needs approval | Anything that changes something
Drafts, record updates, browser clicks. You say yes first.

### Blocked | What it should never touch
Sending, deleting, billing, anything your admin rules out.

notes:
Connectors run on MCP, the Model Context Protocol. Think of it as the USB port for AI: one plug, and any app that speaks it shows Claude its tools. Every connector, and the browser extension, lets you set each tool to always allow, needs approval, or blocked. Start strict. Let it read freely, make it ask before it changes anything, and block the things that can't be undone. Loosen up only as it earns trust.

## Connect the tools you already sell with

### CRM | HubSpot or Salesforce
Account history before every call, notes after it.

### Email and calendar | Gmail, Outlook
Read the thread, draft the reply, find the meeting.

### Prospecting | Apollo
Lists, enrichment and sequences, from inside the chat.

### Calls and team | Zoom, Fireflies, Slack
Transcripts in, recaps out, deal channels kept current.

notes:
Here's the map for a seller. CRM first, because that's where the truth about an account lives. HubSpot has a native connector. Salesforce and Apollo you can reach through Composio, which is next. Email and calendar second. Then whatever you prospect with and whatever records your calls. If your company uses something not on this slide, the question is the same: does it have a connector, and if not, can Composio or the browser reach it.
