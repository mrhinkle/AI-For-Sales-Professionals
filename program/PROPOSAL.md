Here's what I've noticed talking to sales teams this year. Everybody has an AI subscription. Almost nobody is using it to sell. They use it to clean up an email now and then, and they've quietly decided that's all it's good for.

I don't think that's a tools problem. I think nobody has shown them the five or six specific things it's good at in a seller's week, on their own accounts, with their own voice. That's what I do in this session.

I'm not a vendor. I'm not selling you software. I've spent thirty years in enterprise tech, through the dot-com run, open source and cloud, and I sell every day for my own company: sponsorships, tickets, consulting. I use these tools to do it. I'll show your team exactly how, and they'll do it live.

## What your team will walk out with

Not a tour. Each person leaves having done these things on their real accounts during the session:

- **Three files** that turn a generic chatbot into something that knows how they sell: their voice, your ideal customer, your product in the buyer's words.
- **A sourced account brief** on an account they're working, built in ten minutes, with a "why now" sentence they could say out loud.
- **A first-touch email under ninety words** in their own voice, and a sequence shape that gets replies without getting your LinkedIn accounts restricted.
- **A follow-up habit:** prep sheet before the call, recorder on during, summary and CRM notes sent inside ten minutes after.
- **A rehearsal partner** that plays their toughest buyer at eleven at night and tells them what they said wrong.
- **Four guardrails** that keep customer data, your brand, and their jobs safe.
- **One workflow** they commit to running every day for two weeks, and three numbers to measure it.

One rule runs through all of it: the AI drafts, your people send. Nothing goes out a human didn't read.

## Three ways to run it

**The primer. 60 minutes, remote or in person.** The one rule, where AI fits, the three files, research and outreach by live demo, calls, guardrails, first week. Good as a kickoff or for a team that hasn't started. Nobody builds anything in an hour; they see it built and leave with the prompts.

**The workshop. Half a day, in person preferred.** Everything in the primer, but your people do it. Laptops open, real accounts, exercises after every module, and I walk the room. Proposals and pipeline hygiene in brief. This is the version I'd pick for most teams.

**The full day, plus a follow-up.** Every module with exercises, pair role-play, and a proposal first draft on a live deal. Four weeks later we get back on a call, look at the three numbers, and fix whatever stalled. This is for a team that's decided this is how they're going to work.

## What I need from you

- A room or a video link, and your team with laptops and their company AI seat logged in. Any assistant works. I'll tell you ahead of time if the one you use needs a setting changed.
- A 15-minute call with you or the sales leader a week before, so the examples are your buyers and your tools, not generic ones.
- Each person brings five emails they're proud of and three accounts they're working. That's the whole pre-work.
- A yes on recording calls with consent, if your team doesn't already.
