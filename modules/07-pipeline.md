# Keep the pipeline honest

CRM notes that write themselves, and a second opinion on every deal.

notes:
This is the module for anyone who has ever updated {{crm}} at 4:55 on a Friday. Also the one managers like most.

## Ask it to be the skeptic in your deal review

```prompt
Here are my notes, emails and call summaries for the [Company] deal. [paste]
Act as a tough sales manager. Tell me: what we actually know versus what I'm assuming; who the real
decision maker is and whether I've talked to them; what could kill this deal in the next 30 days;
the one action that most improves our odds this week. Be blunt. Don't tell me what I want to hear.
```

notes:
People find this uncomfortable and then they run it on every deal. It is very good at spotting the deal that's all enthusiasm and no economic buyer. Run it before the pipeline review, not after, so you walk in with the answer.

## Hygiene without the Friday afternoon

- CRM notes come out of the follow-up prompt. Paste them in while the email sends
- Weekly summary: paste the week's notes, ask for wins, risks, asks, and what you need from your manager
- Forecast commentary: ask for the one-line reason behind every stage change
- If the CRM has its own assistant, let it do the summaries. Keep the thinking in yours

notes:
The weekly summary prompt takes two minutes and makes your manager's week easier, which is a career move dressed up as hygiene. Point at the fourth line: CRM copilots are good at structured summaries. The judgment prompts belong in your general assistant with your three files loaded.
