# Instructions brain dump

Once. Open the chat, click the microphone and talk for three minutes. Then paste the result into Settings > Instructions (on a Team plan, search Settings for "instructions").

```
Here's a brain dump about me and my job. Turn it into clean personal instructions,
under 300 words, as bullets I can paste into Settings.
I'm a [role] at [company]. We sell [product] to [buyer].
Our systems: [CRM for what, email, where quotes and templates live].
Links I use all the time: [portal, docs, knowledge base, demo signup].
What good looks like for me: [goals]. How I like answers: [length, tone, format].
Words and habits to avoid: [anything you never want it to say or do].
```

Read it back, fix what's wrong, and update it whenever your job changes. Check memory once a quarter for anything stale, like an old price or policy.
