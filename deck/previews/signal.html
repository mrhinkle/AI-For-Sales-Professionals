the one rule

The AI drafts. You send.

Everything today hangs off that sentence. Research, email, call prep, follow-up, proposals. The assistant does the reading and the first draft. Your name goes on it, so you read it and you hit send.

where ai fits

What it's good at today, and what it isn't

Good at

Reading and summarizing

A 10-K, forty job postings, a two-hour transcript. Faster than you, and it doesn't get tired. It does miss things, which is why the sources matter.

Good at

First drafts and rehearsal

Email, follow-ups, proposals, and playing the skeptical buyer so you can practice.

Not yet

Knowing your buyer

It has never met them. It doesn't know what was said at the last QBR unless you tell it.

Not yet

Judgment and relationships

When to push, when to walk, who actually signs. That's still you, and it's the paid part.

01

Research an account in ten minutes

The brief you'd want before any first call, with links, from a company name.

// AI for Sales Professionals
THE AIE NETWORK
This is the fastest payback in the whole session. Most reps either skip research or spend an hour on it. Ten minutes, every time, is the goal.

research

Collect, brief, verify, then one sentence on why now

01

Their site, latest news, open job postings, the annual report if public, and the LinkedIn pages for your two or three contacts.

Collect is where most of the ten minutes goes, and it's mostly copy and paste. If your assistant can browse, let it, but still paste the job postings. Hiring is the best public signal of what a company is about to do. Verify is non-negotiable. The brief is a draft, not a fact sheet, until you've clicked the links.

research · the prompt

The account brief prompt

prompt · copy this
Build a one-page account brief on [Company]. Use my ICP and product files.
Sources attached: [paste or link site, news, job posts, filings, contact profiles].

Sections: what they do and how they make money; what changed in the last six months;
what they're hiring for and what that says; who my contacts are and what they care about;
where Linkerd plausibly fits, in their words; three discovery questions; one "why now" sentence.
Cite a source for every factual claim. If you can't source it, mark it UNVERIFIED.
// AI for Sales Professionals
THE AIE NETWORK
Read the last line out loud. UNVERIFIED in capital letters is the whole trick. The model will tell you what it's unsure of if you give it permission. Then the brief becomes a to-do list for your own checking instead of a trap.

your first week

Five days, one habit each

Day 1
Build the three files
Voice, ICP, product. Twenty minutes. Save them where your assistant loads them automatically.
Day 2
Brief three accounts
Ten minutes each. Click the links. Write the "why now" sentence for each.
Day 3
Rewrite one sequence
Three emails, under ninety words each, in your voice. Approve the first send yourself.
Day 4
Record one call
Prep sheet before, recorder on, follow-up and CRM notes sent inside ten minutes.
Day 5
Review your toughest deal
Run the skeptic prompt. Take the one action it names.
