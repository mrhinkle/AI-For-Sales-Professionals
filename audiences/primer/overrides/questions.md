## Getting started

### Q1 | Should we tell people we use AI?
Decide as a team. I say so in my signature. You still own every send.

### Q2 | We're on Google Workspace. Why not Gemini?
Either can work. Claude's connectors are ahead; Gemini is strong on images.

### Q3 | My settings look different from yours.
Team admins control some settings. Search Settings for "instructions" or "memory."

### Q4 | Can we connect Gmail and Drive?
Ask your admin which connectors are approved. Keep sensitive folders out until then.

notes:
On disclosure: customers are starting to ask vendors for their AI stance, so have one. Mine is a line in my email signature. On Gemini: if your company lives in Google, Gemini is a fine tool and its images and slides are good. I use Claude because its tooling and integrations have been ahead. On settings: Team and Enterprise plans hide or move some options, so use the search box. On connectors: that's your admin's call, and the right call for sensitive folders is to wait for a yes.

## Building workflows

### Q5 | Where do we start, skills or setup?
Setup. Desktop app, approved connectors, a shared Drive, then skills.

### Q6 | Marketing has a brand voice file. Is that a skill?
It's context. A skill says how to use it: copy, don't overwrite, where to save.

### Q7 | Do I have to call the skill by name?
No. Ask for the task. It picks the skill and triggers more reliably with use.

### Q8 | Can it plan our customer outreach every month?
Yes, as a scheduled task. It preps the list and drafts. You send.

notes:
The order matters: connectors and a shared Drive first, because a skill that can't reach your templates can't do much. A brand voice file is great context, so keep it, and add a skill that says how to use it, like "copy the deck, fill in the presenter name, save it to my folder." Skills trigger from plain requests like "make a new quote," and they get more reliable as memory fills in. Monthly outreach is a scheduled task: it does the prep, you pull the trigger.
