# Your first week

One workflow, every day, for two weeks. Then add the next one.

notes:
Don't try to do all of this Monday. Pick one. The research brief is the usual winner because it pays off on the very first call.

## Five days, one habit each

### Day 1 | Build the three files
Voice, ICP, product. Twenty minutes. Save them where your assistant loads them automatically.

### Day 2 | Brief three accounts
Ten minutes each. Click the links. Write the "why now" sentence for each.

### Day 3 | Rewrite one sequence
Three emails, under ninety words each, in your voice. Approve the first send yourself.

### Day 4 | Record one call
Prep sheet before, recorder on, follow-up and CRM notes sent inside ten minutes.

### Day 5 | Review your toughest deal
Run the skeptic prompt. Take the one action it names.

notes:
Point, don't read. Each day is a module from today. By Friday they've done every workflow once on real accounts. Then they pick the one that paid off most and make it daily.

## Measure three things, nothing fancy

- Hours back per week. Guess honestly on Friday afternoon
- Reply rate on the rewritten sequence against the old one
- Follow-ups sent the same day, as a percentage of calls

notes:
Three numbers on a sticky note. If hours back isn't at least three after two weeks, something's wrong with the setup, usually the three files. If the reply rate didn't move, the trigger line isn't landing. Same-day follow-ups is the one managers should watch.
