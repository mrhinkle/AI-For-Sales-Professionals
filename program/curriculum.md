# Curriculum: AI for Sales Professionals

The program teaches sellers to use the general-purpose assistant they already have (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot) plus a meeting recorder to take over the half of the week that isn't selling: research, drafting, prep, follow-up, proposals and CRM. One rule runs through every module: the AI drafts, you send.

## Outcomes

By the end of the full program a participant has, on their own real accounts:

1. Built the three files an assistant reads before every task: voice, ICP, product.
2. Produced a sourced one-page account brief and a "why now" sentence in ten minutes.
3. Drafted a first-touch email under ninety words in their own voice, and knows what to automate in a sequence and what stays manual.
4. Walked into a call with a prep sheet and sent the follow-up, CRM notes and tasks inside ten minutes of hanging up.
5. Rehearsed objections against an assistant playing their toughest buyer.
6. Drafted a proposal from a template and a transcript with every number left for a human.
7. Run a deal risk review and a weekly summary.
8. Knows the four guardrails: what never goes in, use the company seat, check before you trust, it never sends.
9. Leaves with one workflow to run daily for two weeks and three numbers to measure it.

## Three shapes

**Primer, 60 minutes, remote or in person.** Modules 00 to 05, 08, 09, 99 with the exercise slides removed and one live demo thread (research, outreach, follow-up) on a real target account. Best as a first session for a team that hasn't started, or as a kickoff before the workshop.

**Workshop, half day (about 3 hours with a break).** Every module at half length with the hands-on exercises on participants' own accounts. Proposals and pipeline are covered in brief. Participants need a laptop, their assistant open, and three accounts they're working.

**Full day (about 6 hours with breaks and lunch) plus a 30-day follow-up.** Every module at full length with exercises, pair role-play, and a proposal draft on a live deal. The follow-up call four weeks later reviews the three measures and fixes what stalled.

## Pre-work

Sent a week ahead: a five-question survey (tools in use, CRM, biggest time sink, one account they'd like to work on, how they'd rate their comfort with the assistant), and a request to bring five emails they're proud of and the login to their company's assistant seat.

## What participants receive

The deck as a PDF and as the HTML file with speaker notes, the prompt pack, the three-file templates, and a link to this repository with instructions for pointing their own assistant at it.
