# First touch

From the account brief and your voice file.

```
Using my voice file and the account brief for [Company], write a first-touch email to [Name, Title].
Rules: under 90 words, plain text, no bullet points, no buzzwords.
Open with the trigger from the brief in one sentence. Ask one plain question about what they're
trying to solve. Offer the docs or 20 minutes, their choice. Close with a line that makes it easy
to say no. Give me three versions with different openings.
```

Pick one. Read it aloud. Cut anything you wouldn't say on the phone. Count the words.

Sequence shape that works: three emails in the same thread on days 0, 3 and 8, the last one asking whether to close it out. LinkedIn steps stay manual tasks; automating connection requests gets company accounts restricted. Stop on reply, meeting, bounce, unsubscribe, or opportunity.
