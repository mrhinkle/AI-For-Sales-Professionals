### 02. The AI drafts. You send.

_Opening_

Say it twice. This is the rule that keeps the whole thing safe and useful at the same time. It isn't because the tools are bad. It's because it's your name and your relationship. If you remember one thing, remember this one.

### 03. What it's good at today, and what it isn't

_Where AI fits in a seller's week_

Keep this honest. The tools are good at volume and language. They are bad at truth they haven't been given and at judgment. Think of a very well-read new hire on their first day: eager, fast, and knows nothing about your accounts until you tell them. Everything we do today is about telling them.

### 04. Your week, with an assistant in the chair next to you

_Where AI fits in a seller's week_

This is the map for the rest of the session. Every row is a module. The pattern is the same in every one: you give it the source material, it drafts, you check and send. Point, don't read.
We'll do the top rows hands-on.

### 05. Research an account in ten minutes

_Research an account in ten minutes_

This is the fastest payback in the whole session. Most reps either skip research or spend an hour on it. Ten minutes, every time, is the goal.

### 06. Collect, brief, verify, then one sentence on why now

_Research an account in ten minutes_

Collect is where most of the ten minutes goes, and it's mostly copy and paste. If your assistant can browse, let it, but still paste the job postings. Hiring is the best public signal of what a company is about to do. Verify is non-negotiable. The brief is a draft, not a fact sheet, until you've clicked the links.

### 07. The account brief prompt

_Research an account in ten minutes_

Read the last line out loud. UNVERIFIED in capital letters is the whole trick. The model will tell you what it's unsure of if you give it permission. Then the brief becomes a to-do list for your own checking instead of a trap.

### 08. Five days, one habit each

_Your first week_

Point, don't read. Each day is a module from today. By Friday they've done every workflow once on real accounts. Then they pick the one that paid off most and make it daily.
